## Word list (validity) — ENABLE

Word validity uses the **ENABLE** word list, which is in the public domain.

## Word frequencies (machine difficulty) — Norvig count_1w

Usage counts that rank words for machine difficulty are from Peter Norvig's `count_1w.txt`
(derived from the Google Web Trillion Word Corpus), used for frequency ranking only.
